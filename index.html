Payment options support

Understand payment options before your visit

The office can help you learn what payment-option information may be available after your consultation. This app does not run credit, collect SSN, collect date of birth, or approve financing.

Step 1: Share your treatment concern

Tell the team whether you are interested in implants, dentures, All-on-4, veneers, or a second opinion.

Step 2: Ask about monthly options

Select Yes or Maybe in the consultation form so the team knows you want payment-option information.

Step 3: Use secure lender links

Any credit application should happen through a secure financing partner link, not inside this demo app.

Important financing note

Final payment options depend on the treatment plan, lender approval, and eligibility. The dentist must examine you before recommending treatment. The office can answer questions and provide secure links when appropriate.
